Game Overview

Ghost of Tsushima is an open-world action adventure set during the Mongol invasion of Japan. You play as Jin Sakai, one of the last surviving samurai on Tsushima Island.

Travel across forests, mountains, villages and ancient temples while uncovering the island's secrets and helping its people survive the invasion.

Fight with the katana, use stealth techniques and master different combat abilities as you decide how Jin will defend his homeland.
