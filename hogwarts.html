Game Overview

Hogwarts Legacy is an open-world action RPG set in the wizarding world. Step into the shoes of a student attending Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry during the 1800s.

Explore Hogwarts, Hogsmeade and the surrounding wilderness while learning spells, brewing potions, taming magical creatures and battling dangerous enemies.

Your choices and abilities help shape your own journey as you uncover a mysterious story hidden within the wizarding world.
